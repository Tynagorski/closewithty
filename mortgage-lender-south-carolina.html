What Matters Here

Lending in South Carolina

Every state has its own habits — who closes the file, which programs come up, and what stalls a deal. These are the ones that come up in South Carolina.

SC

Coastal and second-home purchases

Charleston, Hilton Head and the Grand Strand draw second-home and investment buyers whose income does not fit a standard pay stub. Bank statement, asset depletion and DSCR programs are built for exactly that.

SC

Closing attorneys, not title companies

South Carolina closings run through a real estate attorney. It changes the timeline and who you chase for what, and it is the sort of thing an out-of-state lender learns the week of closing.

South Carolina Mortgage FAQ

Are you licensed to lend in South Carolina?

Yes. Tyler Nagorski is personally licensed in South Carolina under NMLS #2346195, working from the CrossCountry Mortgage branch at 895 Island Park Drive, Suite 202, Daniel Island, SC (Branch NMLS #2413611). CrossCountry Mortgage, LLC (NMLS #3029) is licensed in all 50 states.

What loan programs are available in South Carolina?

Conventional, FHA, VA, USDA, and jumbo financing, plus the NonQM programs most lenders do not carry — bank statement, DSCR, 1099, asset depletion and WVOE. Which one fits depends on how your income is documented, not on how much of it there is.

I was turned down by another lender in South Carolina. Can you help?

Usually. Most lenders carry a handful of products and send a rejection when you do not fit one of them. If there is a path we will find it, and if there is not one yet we will tell you exactly what has to change and how long it should take.
