VA loans in Hampton Roads

Norfolk, Virginia Beach and Newport News mean a large active-duty and veteran population. A VA loan with no down payment and no monthly mortgage insurance is frequently the strongest option on the table, and entitlement can be restored or used more than once.

Virginia Mortgage FAQ

Are you licensed to lend in Virginia?

Yes. Tyler Nagorski is personally licensed in Virginia under NMLS #2346195, working from the CrossCountry Mortgage branch at 895 Island Park Drive, Suite 202, Daniel Island, SC (Branch NMLS #2413611). CrossCountry Mortgage, LLC (NMLS #3029) is licensed in all 50 states.

What loan programs are available in Virginia?

Conventional, FHA, VA, USDA, and jumbo financing, plus the NonQM programs most lenders do not carry — bank statement, DSCR, 1099, asset depletion and WVOE. Which one fits depends on how your income is documented, not on how much of it there is.

I was turned down by another lender in Virginia. Can you help?

Usually. Most lenders carry a handful of products and send a rejection when you do not fit one of them. If there is a path we will find it, and if there is not one yet we will tell you exactly what has to change and how long it should take.
